KR What If Uploader

KR What If Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel KR's What If Explained.

It uploads the channel's own finished videos, Shorts and thumbnails to YouTube, sets their titles, descriptions and tags, and reads basic performance numbers for those videos so the owner can compare thumbnail and title versions.

It is not offered to other people and does not access any other YouTube channel.
