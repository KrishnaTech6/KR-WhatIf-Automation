Title: What If You Woke Up at 5 AM for 30 Days?

Two of you. Same 5 AM alarm. Thirty days.
By day 14, one brain works like it skipped up to two nights of sleep, and doesn't feel a thing. The difference is one number, and it's not on the alarm.

Real numbers in this video:
- 58% of Indians go to sleep after 11 PM (Wakefit Great Indian Sleep Scorecard 2024). Alarm at 5 means about 6 hours of sleep.
- Adults need 7 hours or more (American Academy of Sleep Medicine + Sleep Research Society, 2015).
- People given 6 hours in bed for 2 weeks performed as badly as people who skipped up to 2 full nights of sleep, and barely felt sleepier (Van Dongen et al., Sleep, 2003).
- 351 spots in our genes are linked to being a morning person or a night owl (Jones et al., Nature Communications 2019, ~7 lakh people).
- People whose genes set their sleep 1 hour earlier had a 23% lower risk of depression (Daghlas et al., JAMA Psychiatry 2021).
- Waking at 5 instead of 7 gives 2 extra hours a day: 60 hours a month, if you get the sleep.
