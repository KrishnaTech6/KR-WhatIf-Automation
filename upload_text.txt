=== MAIN ===
Title: What If You Didn't Use Your Phone for 30 Days?

30 days without your phone gives you back 150 hours. Six full days.
But by day two, you're locked out of your own life. And the biggest change happens inside your head.

Real numbers in this video:
- The average Indian spends about 5 hours a day on a smartphone (EY media & entertainment report, 2025). 5 hours × 30 days = 150 hours, about 6 full days.
- In one study, 89% of students had felt their phone vibrate when it hadn't (Drouin et al., 2012): phantom vibration.
- Almost 9 in 10 reserved train tickets in India are now booked online (Indian Railways, 2025-26).
- In a 2025 study (PNAS Nexus), 467 people blocked mobile internet on their phones for 2 weeks. Calls and texts still worked. Only about a quarter kept it up for 10 of the 14 days.
- Their focus improved about as much as being 10 years younger, and 9 in 10 improved on mood, well-being or focus.
- People who quit Facebook for 4 weeks kept using it less even after they came back (Allcott et al., American Economic Review, 2020).
