Your dad never borrowed ₹1. He just signed as your home loan guarantor. Stop paying your EMI and the bank can call him.
Under Indian law (Contract Act, Section 128), a loan guarantor owes exactly what the borrower owes. The bank can go straight to the guarantor, and the guarantor's CIBIL score falls along with yours.
