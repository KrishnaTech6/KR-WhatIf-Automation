EMI not paid? Here's what really happens to your home loan on day 1, day 30, day 31 and day 91, by RBI rules.
Day 1: the auto-debit bounces and you pay a bounce charge (about ₹500 + GST at a big bank). Day 30: recovery calls start, but RBI says agents can only call between 8 AM and 7 PM. Day 31: your loan is tagged SMA-1 and your CIBIL score starts to fall. Day 91: the loan becomes an NPA, and under the SARFAESI Act the bank can move on your home without a court case.
