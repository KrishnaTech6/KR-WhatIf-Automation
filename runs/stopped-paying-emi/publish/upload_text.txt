=== MAIN: What If You Stopped Paying Your EMI?
You stopped paying your EMI. In 91 days, the bank marks you a defaulter, and it doesn't need a court to come for your home.
Here's exactly what happens, day by day, by RBI rules and Indian law. And the scariest part is a paper you signed on day 1.

Real rules in this video:
- An EMI that isn't paid is marked overdue the same night, in the bank's day-end process (RBI, Nov 2021). After 90 days overdue, the loan becomes an NPA.
- Since 2024, lenders can only add a flat penal charge for a missed EMI, with no interest on top of it (RBI, Aug 2023 circular).
- Recovery agents can't call before 8 AM or after 7 PM, and can't threaten or shame you (RBI, Aug 2022).
- Lenders update credit bureaus like CIBIL every two weeks, and credit records are kept for at least 7 years.
- A guarantor owes the same as the borrower (Indian Contract Act, s.128). Under the SARFAESI Act, a bank can take possession of a home after a 60-day notice, without going to court.
- Not repaying is not a crime by itself (Supreme Court, 2019). But a bounced auto-debit mandate can be punished like a bounced cheque, with up to 2 years in jail (Payment and Settlement Systems Act, s.25). Rare, but real.
