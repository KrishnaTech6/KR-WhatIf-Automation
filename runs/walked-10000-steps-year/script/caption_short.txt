Walking 10,000 steps a day for a year won't fix your weight, and the reason is your own body.
On paper, a year of 10,000 steps a day burns enough calories for almost 10 kilos of weight loss. Real walking studies find people lose far less.
Scientists who studied hunter-gatherers in Tanzania found they burn about the same calories a day as office workers. And the 10,000 goal? It started as a 1960s pedometer name.
