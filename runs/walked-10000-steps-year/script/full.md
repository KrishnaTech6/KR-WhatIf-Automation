# What If You Walked 10,000 Steps Every Day for a Year? — Script

Defaults used (autopilot): **Global** audience · **English** · **4–5 min** · **memes (2, visual only)**. Topic picked by Krishna (#1, last video's tease).

## 1. Package

Verdict: a very searched topic ("10000 steps a day", "10000 steps benefits"), but most videos are fitness-vlog "I tried it" diaries. Our angle: a full year, in real numbers, told as *your* story, with two twists: the scale barely moves, and the thing that actually grows is your brain.

**Myth busted:** "10,000 steps is a medical target" → it was a pedometer's product name.
**Twist:** walking barely changes your weight, but it physically grows the memory part of your brain.
**Effort beat count:** 9 studies (Althoff, Lee, Buffey, Ding, Strain, Richardson, Pontzer, Erickson, plus the 2026 Sydney/UK Biobank steps study used for checking).
Fact-check agent: 10 pass, 2 fixed (time per day → "1.5 to 2 hours"; weight → "roughly 2.5").

## 3. Structure, voice and sound

Countdown ladder through one year: Day 1 → Week 1 → Month 1 → Month 3 → Month 6 → Day 365, then the brain twist. Told as one story: you, a desk worker anywhere, make a New Year promise. English, global audience, memes on (2, visual only).
