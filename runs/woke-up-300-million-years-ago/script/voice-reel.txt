TITLE: What If You Woke Up 300 Million Years Ago? — Reel
LANG: en
KEY_BEATS: hook
HARD_CUT_BEFORE:

[Hook | dramatic]
Your India? Not hot. Frozen.

[Ice | wonder]
Three hundred million years ago.
All the land on Earth was crashing together.
One giant piece.
And India was stuck far down south... under thick ice.
The proof is still in Odisha.
Big boulders that only moving ice could carry.

[Meanwhile | wonder]
Meanwhile, in the middle of the Earth... steaming jungles.
Trees ten storeys tall. Dragonflies with arm-long wings.

[Tease | soft]
Later, India got jungles of its own.
And those jungles never really left. (beat)
{warm} You probably used one today. Can you guess how?
Full story on YouTube. K R's What If Explained.
Because once upon a time... your India was frozen.
