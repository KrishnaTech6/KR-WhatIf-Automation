TITLE: What If You Woke Up 300 Million Years Ago? — Reel
LANG: en

[Hook | dramatic]
Your India, three hundred million years ago? Not tropical. Frozen.

[Gondwana | wonder]
India was part of a giant continent, Gondwana.
And it sat far to the south, under polar ice.
The ice left proof.
In Odisha, the Talchir boulder beds were dropped by glaciers.

[Meanwhile | wonder]
Meanwhile, near the equator... swamp forests of ten-storey clubmosses.
Dragonflies as wide as your arms.

[Tease | soft]
Later, India's frozen land grew forests of its own.
Forests that never really left us. (beat)
{warm} Guess where they are right now?
