Your India, three hundred million years ago? Not hot. Frozen.
All the land on Earth was crashing into one giant piece.
And India was stuck far down south... under thick ice.
The proof is still in Odisha. Big boulders that only moving ice could carry.
Meanwhile, in the middle of the Earth... steaming jungles.
Trees ten storeys tall. Dragonflies with arm-long wings.
Later, India got jungles of its own.
And those jungles never really left.
You probably used one today. Can you guess how?
