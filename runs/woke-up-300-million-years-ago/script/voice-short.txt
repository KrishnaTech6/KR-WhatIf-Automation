[Giants | neutral]
This is Earth, three hundred million years ago.
No dinosaurs. Not even close.

{playful} It eats plants. Probably.

[Why big | neutral]
So why is everything so big?
The air. It's almost one-third oxygen.
Insects don't have lungs. Air seeps in through tiny tubes.
The classic idea: more oxygen, bigger bugs.
Scientists still argue about it.

[Danger | dramatic]
But that same air has a dark side.
One lightning strike... and even damp forests keep burning.
Coal from this time is full of fossil charcoal.
And the scariest thing about this world?
It never really went away.
