[Hook | dramatic]
Don't move. There's a seventy centimetre bug on your chest.
It's a dragonfly. Its wings are as long as your arm.

[Where | neutral]
You just woke up three hundred million years ago.
No dinosaurs. They're not even born yet.
Look down. A millipede... longer than your bed.
{playful} It probably eats plants. Probably.

[Why | neutral]
So why is everything so huge?
The crazy part? It's the air.
It has way more oxygen than ours. Almost one-third.
Bugs don't have lungs. Air just leaks in through tiny tubes.
More oxygen... bigger bugs. That's the big idea.

[Danger | dramatic]
But that air has a dark side.
One lightning strike... and even damp plants keep burning.
And the scariest part? That world never really went away.
It's still in your house.
