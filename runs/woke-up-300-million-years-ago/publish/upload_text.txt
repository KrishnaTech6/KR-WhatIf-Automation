=== MAIN TITLE ===
What If You Woke Up 300 Million Years Ago?

=== MAIN DESCRIPTION ===
You open your eyes and a dragonfly with 70 cm wings is sitting on your chest.
The air is almost one-third oxygen, and one spark can set the whole forest on fire.

Real science in this video:
- 300 million years ago, oxygen was around 30% of the air (today it's 21%).
- Giant clubmoss trees stood about 30 m tall, and there were no flowers, grass or birds yet.
- Griffinflies like Meganeura had wings about 70 cm across; Arthropleura, a millipede-like giant, was about 2.6 m long.
- The land that became India sat far to the south under ice; glacial boulder beds in Odisha (Talchir) still show it.
- Buried swamp forests became coal, and coal makes about three-quarters of India's electricity today.
