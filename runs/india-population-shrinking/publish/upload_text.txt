=== MAIN: What If India's Population Started Shrinking?
Your nani's family photo probably had five or six kids in it. Yours has two, maybe one.
Keep going like that, and India starts to shrink. Here's what that looks like, decade by decade.

Real numbers in this video:
- India's fertility rate is 1.9 children per woman (SRS 2024), below the 2.1 a country needs to stay the same size, for the fifth year in a row.
- India is still growing because of population momentum. The UN expects a peak of about 170 crore in the early 2060s, then about 150 crore by 2100.
- A 2020 Lancet study projected India could fall as low as about 109 crore by 2100.
- By 2046, India is expected to have more people over 60 than children under 15; by 2050, about 1 in 5 Indians will be over 60 (UNFPA India Ageing Report 2023).
- Delhi's fertility rate is 1.2, about the same as Japan's 1.15. Kerala and Tamil Nadu are at 1.3.
