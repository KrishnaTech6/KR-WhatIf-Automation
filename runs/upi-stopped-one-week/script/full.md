Defaults used (no answers needed): India · English · 4–5 min · no memes. Style: **collage**. Verticals: **bold captions**.

## Full script

### Hook (0:00–0:13)

> Ten rupees. One chai. And your phone says... payment failed. *(beat)*
>
> Not just you. 82 crore payments a day... gone. For seven days.
>
> And the person hit hardest... isn't you.

- **Tone:** Tense, close.
- **Scenes:** (1) Frame 0: paper cut-out phone over a chai glass, red stamp "PAYMENT FAILED" slams on the screen. (2) Typed headline strip: "82 CRORE PAYMENTS/DAY — STOPPED"; a counter freezes. (3) A cut-out map of India with QR stickers popping off cities. (4) A dark silhouette of a vegetable cart, red marker question mark.
- **Sound:** Ticking + heartbeat; error buzz on the stamp.
- **On-screen text:** "PAYMENT FAILED"; "82 CRORE / DAY"

### Promise - logo (0:15–0:26)

> This is KR's What If Explained. Real numbers, simple words.
>
> UPI has stumbled before. In April 2025, payments kept failing for about five hours.
>
> Let's stretch that to one full week.

- **Tone:** Neutral.
- **Scenes:** (1) KR logo sting. (2) Newspaper clipping "UPI DOWN — 12 APRIL 2025", a clock cut-out spins 5 hours. (3) Tape stretches the clock into a 7-day calendar strip.
- **Sound:** Logo sting, paper rip.
- **On-screen text:** "12 APR 2025 · ~5 HOURS" → "7 DAYS"

### Minute one - the chai stall (0:27–0:50)

> Minute one. You scan the QR code again. Failed.
>
> You switch apps. Google Pay. Then PhonePe. Then Paytm. Failed, failed, failed.
>
> Here's the myth. Most of us think these apps are UPI.
>
> They're not. They're just doors.
>
> Almost eight out of every ten UPI payments go through just two of these apps.
>
> Behind every door is the same house... UPI, run by NPCI.
>
> Change the door all you want. The house is closed.

- **Tone:** Playful myth-bust, deadpan last line.
- **Scenes:** (1) Card: MINUTE 1 · The chai stall. (2) Three app-icon cut-outs each get a red ✗ stamp. (3) Myth card "THE APPS ARE UPI?" stamped "MYTH". (4) Three paper doors, red string leads behind all three to one house labelled "UPI · NPCI", "CLOSED" sign swings. (5) Pie of two big slices: ~78%.
- **Sound:** Plucks; three error buzzes; door slam.
- **On-screen text:** "8 IN 10 = 2 APPS"; "UPI = NPCI"

### Hour three - the ATM line (0:52–1:20)

> Hour three. Everyone has the same idea. Cash.
>
> The line outside your nearest ATM is now twenty people long. Then fifty.
>
> Here's why. On a normal day, UPI moves about one lakh crore rupees.
>
> That's money that never had to be counted, carried, or kept in a drawer.
>
> Now all of it wants to be paper. By lunch, the machine says... temporarily out of service.
>
> Some people just go home. Hungry. Holding a phone full of money they can't use.

- **Tone:** Tense, then flat.
- **Scenes:** (1) Card: HOUR 3 · The ATM line. (2) Cut-out people pile up into a long queue; counter 20 → 50. (3) "₹1 LAKH CRORE / DAY" headline; a stack of notes taller than the ATM. (4) ATM screen sticker "TEMPORARILY OUT OF SERVICE". (5) One figure walking away, phone glowing. (Dadi's steel dabba sits quietly on a kitchen shelf in a window behind.)
- **Sound:** Tense pulse; crowd murmur; ATM beep.
- **On-screen text:** "₹1 LAKH CRORE / DAY"

### Night one - the shops (1:22–1:50)

> Night one. Your card mostly still works. So does net banking.
>
> UPI is one road. It's just the busiest one.
>
> About 85% of India's digital payments go through it.
>
> But walk into your local kirana. There's a QR sticker on the counter.
>
> And no card machine anywhere.
>
> Quick question. How much cash is in your wallet right now? Be honest. Tell me in the comments.

- **Tone:** Neutral, warm on the question.
- **Scenes:** (1) Card: NIGHT 1 · The shops. (2) Paper road map: one fat road "UPI", thin roads "CARDS", "NET BANKING". (3) Bar: 85% of digital payments. (4) Kirana counter cut-out, QR sticker circled in red marker, empty spot where a card machine would be. (5) KR cut-out holding an open wallet; comment prompt.
- **Sound:** Night ambience; marker squeak.
- **On-screen text:** "85% OF DIGITAL PAYMENTS"; comment question

### Day three - chhutta (1:52–2:35)

> Day three. A new problem. Change.
>
> Last year, the average UPI payment at a shop was about six hundred rupees. Lots are far smaller. A chai. An auto. A bunch of dhaniya.
>
> For years, UPI made exact change. One rupee? Done.
>
> Now every shopkeeper says the same line. Chhutta nahi hai.
>
> And remember the person hit hardest? Meet him.
>
> The sabziwala at your corner. His customers stopped carrying cash years ago.
>
> Today, they walk past him. Not because they don't want his tomatoes. Because they can't pay.

- **Tone:** Neutral, playful on "Chhutta nahi hai", then quiet.
- **Scenes:** (1) Card: DAY 3 · Chhutta. (2) ₹600 price tag, then tiny tags: chai, auto, dhaniya. (3) A ₹500 note cut-out vs a ₹10 chai; stamp "NO CHANGE". (4) Speech bubble on a torn strip: "CHHUTTA NAHI HAI". (5) The sabziwala's cart with tomatoes, people cut-outs walk past.
- **Sound:** Plucks; coin clink; street ambience fades.
- **On-screen text:** "AVG SHOP PAYMENT ≈ ₹600"; "CHHUTTA NAHI HAI"

### Day five - the slowdown (2:38–3:15)

> Day five.
>
> Five days without UPI means about five lakh crore rupees that didn't move the normal way.
>
> The delivery rider who gets paid every evening... didn't. *(beat)*
>
> The cook who sends money home to her village... couldn't.
>
> This is the moment you realise... UPI isn't an app.
>
> It's how India breathes.
>
> So small shops do something very old. They open a notebook. Udhaar. Pay me when it comes back.
>
> India's oldest payment app... is back.

- **Tone:** Deep, slow; deadpan last line.
- **Scenes:** (1) Hard silence, then card: DAY 5 · The slowdown. (2) Counter to ₹5 LAKH CRORE, red string from it to small figures. (3) Delivery rider cut-out, empty phone. (4) A cook and a village house far apart, a dashed line breaks. (5) India map breathing in and out with dots, then going still. (6) A khata notebook with handwritten "udhaar" entries.
- **Sound:** Silence → dark drone + wind; pen scratch on the notebook.
- **On-screen text:** "₹5 LAKH CRORE"; "UDHAAR"

### Day seven - the twist (3:17–3:58)

> Day seven. And here's the twist.
>
> The cash never left.
>
> Right now, India has about ₹43 lakh crore in notes and coins.
>
> And it's growing at its fastest pace in five years. *(beat)*
>
> Even while UPI exploded, we quietly kept more cash than ever.
>
> Not to spend. Just in case.
>
> Your dadi's steel dabba of notes in the kitchen? Turns out she was right.
>
> And for people without a smartphone, there's even UPI 123PAY. It works on a basic phone. No internet needed.

- **Tone:** Wonder, playful on dadi.
- **Scenes:** (1) Card: DAY 7 · The twist. (2) Headline strip "THE CASH NEVER LEFT". (3) Counter ₹43 LAKH CRORE, growth arrow stamped "FASTEST IN 5 YEARS". (4) Two lines rising together: UPI and cash. (5) Kitchen shelf, a steel dabba opens, notes inside, stamp "DADI WAS RIGHT". (6) A basic keypad phone cut-out with "UPI 123PAY · NO INTERNET".
- **Sound:** Hopeful build; tin lid pop.
- **On-screen text:** "₹43 LAKH CRORE IN CASH"; "UPI 123PAY"

### Payoff - back at the chai stall (4:00–4:35)

> Monday. Day eight. UPI comes back.
>
> You walk to the same chai stall. Same QR code. Ten rupees.
>
> Scan. And that tiny sound... payment successful.
>
> India handles nearly half of all the real-time payments on Earth.
>
> And it all depends on that one little sound.
>
> So tell me... after this, would you keep more cash at home?
>
> Next time: what if petrol became free in India? *(beat)*
>
> But that story... is for the next What If.

- **Tone:** Warm, then ominous tease.
- **Scenes:** (1) Card: DAY 8. (2) Same chai stall, same framing as frame 0; green tick stamp "PAYMENT SUCCESSFUL". (3) World map with India's half circled in red marker. (4) KR cut-out with the comment question. (5) Tease card: a petrol pump nozzle with a "₹0" price tag. (6) End card.
- **Sound:** Soft piano; the success chime; ominous rumble on the tease.
- **On-screen text:** "PAYMENT SUCCESSFUL"; "~HALF OF WORLD'S REAL-TIME PAYMENTS"; "NEXT: PETROL FREE?"

## Visual approach

- **Style: collage** (Paper Collage). This is about our money, our shops and what happens to us, so newspaper headlines, rubber stamps ("PAYMENT FAILED", "OUT OF SERVICE", "DADI WAS RIGHT") and red string from cause to effect fit it best. Could also be **atlas** (how UPI works), but the story is about people, not the tech, and the last video was atlas.
- **Short/Reel captions: bold (Style 2)**. The Short is number-led, and the last video used standard captions.
- KR moments: logo/promise, myth-bust (doors), comment question (wallet), dadi joke, outro.
- Visual threads: (1) the chai stall + QR at frame 0 returns in the payoff, same framing; (2) a red-✗ QR sticker sits in every shop scene as the "UPI down" symbol; (3) dadi's steel dabba appears quietly on a kitchen shelf in Hour 3, before the twist.
- Chapter cards: MINUTE 1 · The chai stall / HOUR 3 · The ATM line / NIGHT 1 · The shops / DAY 3 · Chhutta / DAY 5 · The slowdown / DAY 7 · The twist / DAY 8 · Back at the stall.
