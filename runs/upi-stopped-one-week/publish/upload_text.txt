Title: What If UPI Stopped Working for One Week?

Ten rupees, one chai, and your phone says: payment failed.
Now imagine that for 82 crore UPI payments a day, for a whole week.

Real numbers in this video:
- In October 2026, UPI handled about 82 crore payments and about ₹1 lakh crore every single day (NPCI data).
- Almost 8 in 10 UPI payments go through just two apps, PhonePe and Google Pay. The apps are front doors; UPI itself is run by NPCI.
- UPI carries about 85% of India's digital payments by volume (RBI).
- On 12 April 2025, UPI payments kept failing for about five hours.
- India still holds about ₹43 lakh crore in notes and coins, and it's growing at its fastest pace in five years (RBI).
- UPI 123PAY lets you pay with UPI from a basic phone, without internet.
