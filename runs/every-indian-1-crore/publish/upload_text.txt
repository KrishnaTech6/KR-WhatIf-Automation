=== MAIN TITLE ===
What If Every Indian Got ₹1 Crore Tomorrow?

=== MAIN DESCRIPTION ===
8 AM. Every Indian gets ₹1 crore, tax free, even every baby. By month three, your crore buys what about ₹2 lakh used to, and the person who loses the most is sitting in your own house.
Why can't the RBI just print money and make everyone rich? Here's what would happen, hour by hour.

Real numbers in this video:
- India has about 146 crore people (UN, 2025).
- ₹1 crore is about 50 years of income for the average Indian (MoSPI per-capita net national income).
- All the money in India (cash, bank accounts, FDs) is about ₹315 lakh crore. Giving everyone a crore makes it about 47 times bigger in one morning (RBI data).
- NITI Aayog counted about 77 lakh gig workers in 2020-21.
- At its worst, in Nov 2008, prices in Zimbabwe doubled roughly every day, and it printed a 100 trillion dollar note (Hanke & Bostrom, Johns Hopkins).
- The RBI's inflation target is 4% a year. At that speed, prices double about every 18 years.
