TITLE: What If You Ate Only Maggi for 30 Days? — Short
LANG: en
KEY_BEATS: hook
HARD_CUT_BEFORE:

[Hook | dramatic]
You ate seven Maggi packets a day. For a month. Your face changes first.

[Day one | playful]
Day one feels amazing. Two minutes a packet. Around a hundred rupees a day.

[Day three | dramatic]
Day three. Your face looks puffy. Your rings feel tight.
Because seven packets give you about three times the salt you should eat in a day.
Your body holds on to water just to dilute it.

[Week one | neutral]
Week one. The bathroom stops working. There's almost no fibre in a packet.

[Day ten | neutral]
Day ten. The tiny bacteria in your gut start disappearing. They lived on vegetables. You stopped feeding them.

[Tease | dramatic]
And the scariest part? It starts around day thirty. And it has nothing to do with salt.
